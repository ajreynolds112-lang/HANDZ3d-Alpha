DESIGN A PROFESSIONAL BOXING RING WALK PRESENTATION FOR CAREER MODE

OVERVIEW

Create a polished pre-fight presentation that plays before every Career Mode fight.

The presentation should build anticipation and make every fight feel like a televised professional boxing event.

The presentation should be smooth, cinematic, and skippable.

The player should be able to skip the presentation at any time by pressing the Skip button.

The entire presentation should last approximately 20-35 seconds if not skipped.

-----------------------------------

PRESENTATION FLOW

The sequence should play in the following order:

1. Arena Introduction
2. Blue Corner Ring Walk
3. Blue Corner Fighter Record
4. Red Corner Ring Walk
5. Red Corner Fighter Record
6. Tale Of The Tape
7. Transition into referee instructions
8. Opening bell
9. Fight begins

-----------------------------------

ARENA INTRODUCTION

Before either fighter enters:

Display a wide camera shot of the arena.

Show:

• Ring
• Crowd
• Arena lighting
• Spotlights
• Camera slowly panning across the arena

Hold this shot for approximately 2-3 seconds.

-----------------------------------

BLUE CORNER RING WALK

The Blue Corner fighter walks toward the ring.

Animation should include:

• Walking down the aisle
• Confident movement
• Camera following from different angles
• Crowd visible in the background

Use multiple cinematic cameras such as:

• Front tracking camera
• Side tracking camera
• Slight overhead shot
• Behind-the-fighter shot

The animation should feel energetic but not overly dramatic.

-----------------------------------

BLUE CORNER RECORD

After the Blue Corner reaches the ring:

Display a professional fighter card.

Display for approximately 3 seconds.

Show:

Fighter Name

Nickname

Country

Current Rank

Career Record

Wins

Losses

Draws

KO Wins

If the fighter is the Undisputed Champion:

Display

UNDISPUTED CHAMPION

beneath their name.

-----------------------------------

RED CORNER RING WALK

Repeat the same sequence for the Red Corner fighter.

Use the same camera system.

The second walk should not feel identical.

Slightly vary:

• Camera angles
• Timing
• Camera distance

-----------------------------------

RED CORNER RECORD

Display the same information.

Keep on screen approximately 3 seconds.

-----------------------------------

TALE OF THE TAPE

After both fighters have entered the ring:

Display a full-screen presentation titled:

TALE OF THE TAPE

The Tale Of The Tape should compare both fighters side-by-side.

Include:

Name

Nickname

Country

Age

Height (if available)

Reach (if available)

Current Rank

Record

KO Wins

Fighter Level

Power

Speed

Defense

Stamina

Focus

Display both fighters simultaneously.

The comparison should remain visible for approximately 4-6 seconds.

Use a clean professional broadcast layout.

-----------------------------------

CAMERA WORK

Throughout the presentation:

Use cinematic camera cuts.

Examples:

Wide arena shot

Corner close-up

Walking shot

Low-angle shot

Crowd reaction

Ring ropes

Glove close-up

Face close-up

Do not keep the same camera angle for too long.

-----------------------------------

ANIMATION

Ring walks should feel natural.

The fighter should:

Walk forward naturally.

Occasionally look toward the ring.

Occasionally acknowledge the crowd.

Continue walking without stopping.

Avoid robotic movement.

-----------------------------------

TRANSITION

After the Tale Of The Tape disappears:

Fade smoothly into:

Both fighters inside the ring.

Referee standing between them.

Camera slowly zooms in.

Then:

Bell rings.

Fight begins.

-----------------------------------

SKIP SYSTEM

At any point during the presentation:

Pressing the Skip button immediately skips to:

Both fighters already inside the ring.

Referee ready.

Bell rings.

Fight begins.

-----------------------------------

CAREER IMMERSION

The presentation should make every Career Mode fight feel important.

Even low-ranked fights should receive a professional presentation.

As the player climbs the rankings, the presentation should feel increasingly prestigious without changing the core sequence.

The presentation should feel similar to a modern televised boxing broadcast rather than an arcade game.
