WEEK ADVANCEMENT RULE

• 1 Week passes every 2 player training sessions.
• When week advances → Trigger SimulateWeek().

ROSTER STRUCTURE (151 Fighters)

Each fighter has:

rank

rating_score (ELO-based)

record (W-L)

isChampion (true/false)

isKeyFighter (true/false)

lastFightWeek

activityCooldown (prevents fighting every week)

Less than 8 fighters are isKeyFighter = true.

Key fighters:

Win 100% of simulated fights.

Lose only if they fight the player.

Still gain rating normally from wins.

WEEKLY SIMULATION FLOW
Step 1 — Build Eligible Pool

Exclude:

Player

Player’s scheduled opponent

Anyone who fought last week (optional cooldown rule)

From remaining fighters:

Randomly select 50 fighters.

These 50 enter matchmaking pool.

If odd number, one random fighter sits out.

Step 2 — Pairing Rules

Loop through selected fighters:

For each unpaired fighter:

Find available opponent within ±5 ranks.

Choose closest rank difference first.

Once paired, remove both from pool.

If no opponent within 5 ranks:

Fighter sits out this week.

Every paired fight guarantees:

One win

One loss

This ensures movement in the system.

Step 3 — Win Probability Calculation

For non-key fighters:

Use rating difference to determine win odds:

Higher rating fighter win chance =

50% base

(rating_difference_scaled)

Example logic:

0–50 rating diff → 55% favorite

50–100 diff → 60%

100–200 diff → 70%

200+ diff → 80% cap

Cap maximum at 85% so randomness always exists.

Then:

Roll RNG (0–100).

Determine winner.

Step 4 — Key Fighter Override

If fighter.isKeyFighter == true:

Auto-win.

Skip RNG.

Apply rating gain.

Opponent receives rating loss.

Key fighters can still climb organically through system.

They are protected, not teleported.

Step 5 — Determine Fight Type

After winner decided:

Roll secondary RNG:

60% Close Decision

25% Clear Decision

10% Dominant

5% KO

This determines performance multiplier in ELO update.

Step 6 — Apply Rating Updates

Use your ELO formula:

Apply difficulty multiplier

Apply champion multiplier if relevant

Update both fighters’ rating_score

Update record

Step 7 — Resort Rankings

After ALL fights conclude:

Sort full roster by rating_score descending.

Reassign ranks 1–151.

Ensure exactly 1 champion:

Rank 1 = Champion

If champion loses:

New rank 1 becomes champion automatically.

SPECIAL CASES
Champion Fight Frequency

Champion must fight at least every 4 weeks.

If no player challenge:

Auto-schedule vs highest eligible contender within ±5 ranks.

Freak Upset Logic

If champion loses (not key fighter):

Let rating system handle change naturally.

Do not manually swap ranks.

Sorting handles it.

If Key Fighter Becomes Champion

They remain undefeated until player beats them.

They defend normally through simulation.

SYSTEM STABILITY RULES

• No manual rank swapping.
• No scripted rank jumps.
• Only rating_score determines rank.
• Every simulated fight creates one winner and one loser.
• Rankings evolve weekly from real results.

This creates:

Constant movement.

Realistic churn.

Protected elite threats.

Upsets that matter.

151 fighters feeling alive.
