 came up with 100 Strings the ai should be able to execute, from 3 Segments to 30 maximum. The ai can throw a jab "w", cross "e", left hook "q", right hook "r", left uppercut "s", right uppercut "d," "perfect Block "v" (negates damage for a short duration when timed correctly), charge punch that's armed with "A"and needs to be thrown within one second of arming it, walking in all directions (we can use this to direct the ai to do things like step in and out of range for a pull-counter like Mayweather for example), ducking to avoid head punches with left shift, and switching between orthodox and southpaw with right shift. Each of these inputs will be called Segments, and segments will be connected using what we'll call "beats," beats are the timing in which the ai can adjust the speed of the next segment executing based on the opponents' movements and the ai will adjust them by 0.005 seconds up to 0.5 seconds based on weighted percentages of success/whiff. The ai will be able to decide to add or subtract beats where necessary, and rearrange segment pieces based on what’s worked previously against the player. The segments should have each ai generated use a bias toward certain beats based on the string, but all ai’s should adapt. The adapt chance is 95% for Champion, 90% for elite, 85% for contender, and 65% for Journeyman.
The Input Dictionary (Legend)
* w = Jab | e = Cross | q = Left Hook | r = Right Hook | s = Left Uppercut | d = Right Uppercut
* v = Perfect Block | A = Arm Charge Punch (must be followed by a punch)
* LShift = Duck | RShift = Switch Stance
* Fwd = Walk Forward | Back = Walk Backward | Left = Circle Left | Right = Circle Right
* ~ = The Beat. (Your dynamic 0.005s - 0.5s delay. The AI will stretch this on whiffs, or shrink it to 0.005s for rapid-fire success execution).

Implementation Note on the "Beats"
Because you are tracking whiff/success percentages, the real magic happens in how the AI treats the ~. If the AI registers a hit on String #6 (w ~ w ~ q ~ Right), the beats tighten to 0.005s so the combo fires flawlessly. If the first jab whiffs, the AI can stretch the beat to 0.2s, recalculating if the player is moving out of range, potentially canceling the rest of the string to save stamina.
