DESIGN A COMPLETE ELO-BASED RANKING SYSTEM FOR MY BOXING CAREER MODE

OVERVIEW

The game contains a large roster of AI boxers plus one created player.

Current roster:
- 650 AI boxers
- 1 Career Player

The Career Player always starts ranked #651 (last place).

The player must earn every ranking position by defeating opponents.

This ranking system should simulate a living boxing world every in-game week.

DO NOT RANDOMLY SHUFFLE RANKINGS.
Every movement in the rankings should be the result of wins, losses, activity, streaks, or inactivity.

There are NO injuries in this game.

WEEKLY SIMULATION

Each simulated week:

1. Shuffle all eligible fighters.

2. Roughly half the roster fights.

3. Half remain inactive.

4. Nobody fights twice in one week.

5. Champions may fight slightly less often.

6. Fighters should never fight the exact same opponent repeatedly.

Attempt to avoid rematches unless:
- title fight
- rivalry
- 8+ weeks have passed

KEY FIGHTERS

Some fighters are marked:

IsKeyFighter = true

Key Fighters represent the legendary boxers of the era.

Requirements:

• Start with very high Elo.

• Receive slight simulation advantages.

• Almost never lose AI simulated fights.

• May lose to another Key Fighter.

• May lose to the player.

Do NOT make them literally unbeatable.

When two Key Fighters fight,
simulate normally.
