REPLACE THE CURRENT MOVEMENT SYSTEM WITH A TRUE FOOTWORK-BASED MOVEMENT SYSTEM.

The current implementation causes fighters to slide across the canvas. This should no longer happen.

Fighters should never appear to glide. Every movement across the ring should be driven by visible footwork animations.

Movement must always be synchronized with the fighter's position, rhythm system, and facing direction.

--------------------------------------------------

CORE MOVEMENT RULE

The fighter's body should only translate across the ring because their feet are stepping.

Do NOT move the entire fighter sprite smoothly while the feet remain planted.

Instead:

1. Lift the leading stepping foot.
2. Move that foot to the new position.
3. Plant the foot.
4. Immediately lift the trailing foot.
5. Move it forward.
6. Plant it.

Only after these foot placements should the body complete its movement.

Every directional movement should consist of alternating steps.

Every visible movement should contain footwork.

--------------------------------------------------

STEP ORDER

Determine which foot moves first based on:

• movement direction
• current facing direction
• stance (orthodox or southpaw)

Never hardcode left foot or right foot.

Always determine:

Front Foot

Back Foot

relative to the opponent.

The Front Foot is always the foot closest to the opponent.

The Back Foot is always the foot furthest from the opponent.

--------------------------------------------------

ORTHODOX STEP LOGIC

Orthodox fighters begin every fight in this stance.

Forward

Front Foot steps first.

Back Foot follows.

Backward

Back Foot steps first.

Front Foot follows.

Move Left

Front Foot steps first.

Back Foot follows.

Move Right

Back Foot steps first.

Front Foot follows.

--------------------------------------------------

SOUTHPAW STEP LOGIC

Southpaw reverses lateral movement.

Forward

Front Foot first.

Backward

Back Foot first.

Move Left

Back Foot first.

Move Right

Front Foot first.

Forward and backward movement remain identical.

Only lateral movement changes.

--------------------------------------------------

FACING DIRECTION

Never assume fighters face left or right.

The game already calculates facing direction.

Continue using the existing directional facing formulas.

Every frame:

Determine

• current facing angle

• opponent position

• stance

Then calculate:

Front Foot

Back Foot

before selecting the walking animation.

This should remain correct regardless of where fighters are located inside the ring.

--------------------------------------------------

STEP ANIMATION

Every step should contain a small lifting motion.

The stepping leg should:

Lift from the floor.

Travel upward.

Move slightly forward.

Return downward.

Land.

The foot should travel along a shallow reverse parabolic arc.

The lift should only be a few pixels.

Never exaggerate.

The movement should resemble realistic boxing footwork rather than walking.

--------------------------------------------------

LEG ANIMATION

When stepping:

Hip moves slightly.

Knee bends.

Lower leg follows.

Foot remains pointed naturally.

The planted foot should stay grounded until it becomes the stepping foot.

Never slide planted feet.

--------------------------------------------------

BODY WEIGHT SHIFT

Every completed step should shift body weight.

Slightly move:

hips

shoulders

head

center of mass

toward the planted foot.

This creates believable balance.

--------------------------------------------------

BOBBING SYNCHRONIZATION

The existing pendulum bobbing animation must continue.

Walking should synchronize with the bobbing.

As each foot lands:

The bob should reach its lowest point.

As each foot lifts:

The bob rises slightly.

This creates natural rhythm.

--------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------

DUCKING MOVEMENT

Walking while ducking should also use stepping.

The fighter should never glide while crouched.

The crouched body should continue stepping using the same rules.

Leg lift height should simply be smaller.

--------------------------------------------------

GETTING HIT

When a fighter absorbs a clean punch:

Perform a reactive recovery step.

The foot opposite the direction of force should step backward.

This should happen before the body fully recovers.

The recovery animation should preserve balance.

Do not allow the fighter to slide backward.

Every backward displacement must come from an actual recovery step.

Actual movement speed should remain unchanged.

Only the animation method changes.

Distance traveled per second should stay identical.

Movement now appears realistic because it is driven by stepping instead of sliding.
