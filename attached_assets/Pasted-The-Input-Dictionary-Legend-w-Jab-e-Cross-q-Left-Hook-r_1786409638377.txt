The Input Dictionary (Legend)
* w = Jab | e = Cross | q = Left Hook | r = Right Hook | s = Left Uppercut | d = Right Uppercut
* v = Perfect Block | A = Arm Charge Punch (must be followed by a punch)
* LShift = Duck | RShift = Switch Stance
* Fwd = Walk Forward | Back = Walk Backward | Left = Circle Left | Right = Circle Right
* ~ = The Beat. (Your dynamic 0.005s - 0.5s delay. The AI will stretch this on whiffs, or shrink it to 0.005s for rapid-fire success execution).
Category 1: The Fundamentals (Basic Probes & Poking)
Used to test the opponent's timing and reaction speeds.
1. 1-2 Retreat: w ~ e ~ Back
2. Double Jab Cross: Fwd ~ w ~ w ~ e
3. Jab, Duck, Cross: w ~ LShift ~ e ~ Back
4. Circling Jab: Left ~ w ~ w ~ Back
5. Block & Jab: v ~ w ~ Fwd
6. Double Jab, Lead Hook: w ~ w ~ q ~ Right
7. Step In, Hooks: Fwd ~ r ~ q ~ Back
8. Duck to Lead Hook: LShift ~ q ~ e ~ Back
9. Uppercut Probe: w ~ d ~ q ~ Back
10. The Classic 1-2-3: Fwd ~ w ~ e ~ q ~ Back
