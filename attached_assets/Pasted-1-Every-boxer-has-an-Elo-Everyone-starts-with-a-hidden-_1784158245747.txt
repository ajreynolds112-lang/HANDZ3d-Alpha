One addition I'd strongly recommend: "Career Momentum"

Pure Elo is excellent for determining who is strongest, but it doesn't capture who is hot right now. Add a hidden Momentum value (say, -100 to +100) that changes quickly:

Win by KO: +15 to +25
Win by decision: +8 to +12
Upset victory: additional +15
Loss by KO: -20
Decision loss: -10
Inactive week: -2

Momentum decays by about 10% each week. Then, when matchmaking and rankings are close, use a small fraction of Momentum (for example, Ranking Score = Elo + 0.2 × Momentum + other bonuses). This lets a fighter who has rattled off six impressive wins climb faster than someone with the same Elo who's been inactive or barely edging out opponents.

Over a long career, Elo still determines the best fighters. Momentum creates believable hot streaks, surprise contenders, and "everyone's talking about this guy" moments, making the simulated boxing world feel much more dynamic.
